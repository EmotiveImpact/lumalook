Privacy policy.

LumaLook has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Your photos and videos are edited on your iPhone and never sent to us. This page explains the few places where any data moves at all.

Effective 22 November 2026

The short version

  • We don't run accounts, ads, analytics or tracking of any kind.
  • Grading, editing and exporting happen on your iPhone. We never receive your photos, videos or projects.
  • Apple handles payments. A service called RevenueCat confirms what you have bought, using an anonymous ID.
  • Auto captions run on your iPhone whenever your language allows. When it doesn't, Apple transcribes the audio, and the app tells you before it starts.

Who we are

LumaLook is made by Emotive Impact ("we", "us"). We are responsible for the small amount of personal data described here. You can reach us at the support address in the app.

What stays on your iPhone

Your photos, videos, projects, saved looks, imported LUTs, captions and settings are stored on your iPhone, in the app's own storage or in your photo library. LumaLook processes them on the device. We have no servers that receive them and no way to see them.

If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or a computer, the app's data may be included in that backup under Apple's terms. Deleting the app deletes its data from your iPhone.

Permissions and why we ask

iOS asks your permission before LumaLook can use any of these. You can change your answer at any time in Settings, then LumaLook.

PermissionWhat it is for
CameraShooting photos and video with live looks.
MicrophoneRecording sound with your videos and recording voiceovers.
PhotosChoosing photos and clips to grade and edit, and saving what you export.
Speech recognitionTurning the speech in your video into captions, only when you ask for them.

Auto captions

When you ask for captions, LumaLook transcribes the sound of your edit using Apple's speech recognition. On recent versions of iOS, and for languages with an on-device model, this happens entirely on your iPhone and no audio leaves it.

For a language with no on-device model, the audio of your edit is sent to Apple to be transcribed, under Apple's privacy policy. The Captions panel shows which of the two will happen before you start. We never receive the audio or the text.

Purchases

Subscriptions, Lifetime and packs are bought through the App Store. Apple processes the payment, and we never see your card or Apple Account details.

To check what you own across devices and restores, LumaLook uses RevenueCat, a subscription service that works on our behalf. When the app connects, RevenueCat receives:

  • an anonymous ID the app creates, which is not linked to your name or email;
  • your App Store purchase and subscription history for LumaLook;
  • basic technical details such as your device model, iOS version, app version and App Store country;
  • your IP address, as with any connection over the internet.

We use this only to unlock what you have paid for and to understand how many people subscribe. It is never used for advertising and never sold.

Emails to support

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to send, such as a description of a problem or a screenshot. We use it only to reply to you, and we keep it only as long as it is useful for helping you. Ask us at any time and we will delete it.

This website

This website sets no cookies and runs no analytics. Its fonts and images are served from the site itself, not from third parties. Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs, including IP addresses, for a short time to keep the site secure and working.

Children

LumaLook is not directed at children under 13, and because the app has no accounts we do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, children included. If you think a child has emailed us, contact us and we will delete the message.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under UK and EU data protection law, you can ask us what personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, and object to how we use it. Email us and we will reply within one month. We hold very little: usually only support emails and the anonymous purchase record kept by RevenueCat.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office in the UK, or to the data protection authority where you live.

Changes to this policy

If we change how LumaLook handles data, we will update this page and the date at the top. If a change is significant, we will also say so in the app's release notes.
