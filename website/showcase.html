How to get featured

Submissions open when the app launches. Founding Creators can send work from their first build.

01

Shoot it in LumaLook

Photo or video, shot in the camera or graded afterwards. Any look, any pack.

02

Tag #LumaLook

Post it on Instagram or TikTok with the tag, or send it to us once submissions open.

03

We ask first

We'll always ask before we feature anything, and credit you by name, handle and look. Change your mind and it comes down.
