Founding Creators

Help us get your skin right.

For decades, cameras and filters were tuned on light skin, and deep skin came out grey, orange or lost in shadow. LumaLook is built the other way round. We're looking for creators to test it first, push it hard and tell us where it still gets you wrong.

What you get

  • Lifetime, free. Every look and every pack, now and in future.
  • First access. New builds on your iPhone through TestFlight before anyone else.
  • A real say. Vote on new looks and features, and suggest your own.
  • Credit. Your name on our Founding Creators page, if you'd like it there.

What we ask

  • Try to break the looks: backlit, neon, night, indoor light, and group shots with lighter and deeper skin together.
  • A two-minute form after each new build, with before-and-after frames if you're happy to share them.
  • For some, two short video calls over six weeks, so we can see how you edit.

Who it's for

Anyone 18 or over who shoots photos or video on an iPhone, from beauty and fashion to music, weddings, street and everyday life. Everyone is welcome to apply. We especially want to hear from Black and brown creators and people with deep skin, because that's where most tools still fall short.

The first group is small, about 30 people, and we choose for a spread of skin tones, styles and places. If you aren't picked this round, we'll keep your application for the next one unless you tell us not to.
