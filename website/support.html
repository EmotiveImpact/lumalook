Use Help & Support in the app

From the app

Open You, then Settings, then Help & Support. It opens an email to us with the subject filled in.

Restore purchases

New iPhone, or reinstalled the app? Sign in with the same Apple Account you bought with, then open You, Settings and tap Restore Purchases. Pro, Lifetime and any packs you own come back straight away.

Purchases are personal to your Apple Account and are not shared through Family Sharing.

Bought Lifetime on this website? It is tied to the email you paid with. Open You, then Restore web purchase, enter that email and type in the code we send. Lost access to that email? Write to us with your Polar receipt and we will move it.

Cancel or change a plan

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions and LumaLook. From there you can cancel, or switch between monthly and yearly. If you cancel, Pro stays on until the end of the period you have paid for.

To avoid paying for a trial, cancel at least 24 hours before the 7 days end.

Refunds

Apple handles every payment, so refunds are requested at reportaproblem.apple.com. We can't issue refunds ourselves, but if something isn't working we would love the chance to fix it first.

Why is a look locked?

A lock means the look belongs to a paid pack. You can preview it on your own photos and clips as much as you like. To export with it, get its pack or go Pro. Everything in Negative Set 01 and Film Stocks 01 is free to export.

Importing your own LUTs

LumaLook reads .cube LUT files and ZIP files full of them. Open one from the Files app, Mail or AirDrop and choose LumaLook, or open You, Settings, then Imported LUTs and tap the plus button. Imported LUTs sit beside our looks and work in the camera, the grade room and the editor.

Auto captions

Open a project, tap Captions and LumaLook writes word-by-word captions from the speech in your edit. You can fix any word, pick a style and choose whether to burn them into the video.

Captions are worked out on your iPhone whenever your language supports it. If it doesn't, Apple's speech recognition handles them, and the panel tells you before you start. If captions come back empty, check the clip has audible speech and that Speech Recognition is allowed in Settings, then LumaLook.

Exporting

Exports go to your photo library at the quality you choose in Settings, from 720p up to 4K, in H.264 or HEVC. HEVC files are smaller; H.264 plays almost everywhere. Nothing you export has a watermark.

If an export stops part way, make sure your iPhone has free space and keep LumaLook open until it finishes.

Space and backups

Projects live on your iPhone. You can see how much space LumaLook uses in You, Settings, then Storage Used. Your projects are included in your iPhone's iCloud or computer backup; deleting the app deletes them, so export anything you want to keep first.
