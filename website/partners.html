What we stand by

The few rules every partnership follows, whoever it's with.

Creators come first

We only work with partners whose products and values sit well with the people who use LumaLook.

Always labelled

Sponsored looks, packs and features are clearly marked as sponsored, in the app and on this site.

Never your data

LumaLook has no tracking and no accounts. We will never share or sell anything about the people who use it.

Tell us about you

A few lines is plenty. Tell us who you are, what you have in mind and roughly when. We read every enquiry ourselves and reply within five working days.

Good to know

  • Small is fine. Local groups, one-off workshops and first-time sponsors are all welcome.
  • Creators, apply here instead. If you want to test the app, become a Founding Creator.
