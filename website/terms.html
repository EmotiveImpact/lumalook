Subscriptions

  • LumaLook Pro is offered yearly ($34.99) or monthly ($5.99). The App Store shows the price in your currency before you buy.
  • Yearly Pro may include a 7-day free trial. If you do not cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends, it turns into a paid yearly subscription.
  • Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase, or when the trial ends.
  • Subscriptions renew automatically at the same price and length unless you turn off auto-renew at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your account is charged for the renewal within the 24 hours before the period ends.
  • You can manage or cancel in your iPhone's Settings: tap your name, then Subscriptions, then LumaLook. Cancelling stops the next renewal; you keep Pro until the end of the period you have paid for.
  • If we ever change the price, Apple will tell you in advance and, where the law requires it, ask for your agreement.

Lifetime and packs

Lifetime is a single payment that gives you everything in Pro for as long as LumaLook is offered on the App Store, including every pack we release in the future. Packs bought on their own are one-off purchases that stay yours. Neither renews.

Purchases are tied to your Apple Account and are not shared through Family Sharing. Tap Restore Purchases in the app's Settings to bring them back on a new iPhone.
