How it works

One look, from shutter to share.

Pick a look once and it follows your footage through every step: in the viewfinder, on the timeline and in the file you post. Photos and video, in one app.

1.0 Shoot

Shoot it graded.

Choose a look before you press record and watch it live in the viewfinder. It is baked into every clip and photo you take, so what you see is what you get.

  • Live looksSwipe through looks on the live picture and set the strength.
  • Lens rulerDial the focal length from 13 to 48 mm like a zoom ring.
  • Manual controlExposure, white balance, shutter, ISO and focus, with readouts.
  • Four modesPhoto, video, slo-mo and portrait, with a roll counter.

2.0 Grade

Grade anything.

Put a look on any photo or clip in your library, stack another on top, then compare before and after. Bring your own .cube LUTs and they sit beside ours.

  • 123 looks, 11 packsFilm negatives, slide, mono, instant, cinema print and LumaLook originals.
  • Stack looksLayer looks with a strength for each one.
  • Fine-tuneCurves, colour wheels, HSL, split tone, grain and halation.
  • Your own LUTsOpen .cube files or ZIP packs straight from the Files app.

3.0 Edit

Edit it together.

A full video editor that keeps the look on every clip. Trim, add music and captions, and cut to the beat without leaving the app.

  • A look per clipGive each clip its own look, or one look to all.
  • Auto captionsWord-by-word captions from your speech, worked out on your iPhone where it can.
  • Music and beatsDrop in a track and cut your clips to its beat.
  • Speed, text and moreSpeed curves, transitions, text, voiceover, green screen and picture in picture.

4.0 Export

Export it clean.

No watermark on anything you make, on the free plan or any other. Save to your camera roll at up to 4K, ready for Reels, TikTok and Shorts.

  • No watermarkFree or paid, your work carries only your name.
  • Up to 4KH.264 or HEVC from 720p to 4K, plus GIF for loops.
  • Before and afterDrag a wipe across the frame before you commit.
  • Take your gradeExport your grade as a .cube to use in other editors, with Pro.

Questions

Good to know.

Anything else, ask us. A real person answers.

Is LumaLook free?

Yes. The camera, the grade room, the video editor and two full packs (19 looks) are free to use and export, with no watermark. You can preview every other look on your own photos and clips before you decide to buy anything.

What does "skin-true" mean?

Our looks are built and checked on Black and brown skin first, then everyone else. In practice that means warmth in faces is protected, shadows keep their detail, and skin lands on the vectorscope's skin-tone line instead of drifting grey or orange.

Does it work for photos and video?

Both. The same look works in the camera, on photos in your library and on every clip in the editor, so a shoot can match from stills to video.

Can I use my own LUTs?

Yes. Open .cube files or ZIP packs of them from the Files app and they appear in your library beside ours. With Pro you can also export any grade you build as a .cube.

Are my photos and videos uploaded anywhere?

No. Grading, editing and exporting all happen on your iPhone. Auto captions run on the device whenever your language supports it; otherwise Apple's speech recognition service handles them. The privacy policy has the details.

How does the free trial work?

Yearly Pro starts with 7 days free. If you cancel at least 24 hours before the week ends, you pay nothing. Otherwise it renews at $34.99 a year (or your local price) until you cancel in Settings, then your name, then Subscriptions.

Which iPhones does it run on?

Any iPhone on iOS 17 or later. Some camera features depend on the lenses your iPhone has.

I changed phones. How do I get my purchases back?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, open You, then Settings, and tap Restore Purchases. Pro, Lifetime and any packs you bought come straight back.
